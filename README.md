## What it does

Pipeline Forecast AI scores every open deal on real close probability — from engagement, activity, and historical patterns — giving you a forecast you can defend in the boardroom.

## Key features

- **Deal-level predictions** — per-deal close probability with top risk factors.
- **Commit accuracy tracking** — reps' calls measured against outcomes.
- **Pipeline coverage radar** — coverage gaps flagged per segment and quarter.
- **Slippage alerts** — deals going quiet before they push.
- **Scenario planning** — best/likely/worst quarter outcomes modeled.

## Who it's for

CROs and RevOps who need a forecast built on evidence, not optimism.
